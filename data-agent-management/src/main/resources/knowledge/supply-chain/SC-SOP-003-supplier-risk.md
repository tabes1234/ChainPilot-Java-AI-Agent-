# Supplier Risk Response

Source: SC-SOP-003, revision 1.0

Review on-time delivery, defect rate, and open incidents together. A high supplier risk requires a recovery plan, named supplier contact, and evaluation of an approved backup source. A medium risk requires capacity confirmation and closer milestone tracking. A low risk with no open incidents remains on routine monitoring. Never substitute an unapproved supplier without procurement authorization.

Keep the supplier name, risk level, observed delivery performance, and incident evidence in the analysis report.

关键词：供应商风险、履约率、缺陷率、供应商事件、恢复计划、备选供应商。
