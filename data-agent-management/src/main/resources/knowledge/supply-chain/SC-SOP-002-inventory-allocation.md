# Inventory Allocation and Shortage Handling

Source: SC-SOP-002, revision 1.0

Available-to-promise inventory is on-hand quantity minus reservations and safety stock. Do not allocate safety stock to a customer order without an authorized exception. When an order quantity exceeds available-to-promise quantity, record the shortage and check alternate locations, approved substitutions, and replenishment dates. Allocation changes require an inventory owner and must preserve existing customer commitments.

Risk reports should show the SKU, required quantity, available-to-promise quantity, and shortage so the recommendation can be checked.

关键词：库存、可承诺库存、安全库存、预留量、库存短缺、补货。
