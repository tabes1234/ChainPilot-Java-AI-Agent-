# Order Fulfillment and Delay Escalation

Source: SC-SOP-001, revision 1.0

When the estimated delivery date is later than the requested delivery date, mark the order as at risk and confirm a recovery date with the supplier. Notify the customer using the confirmed date. Do not promise a new date before supplier confirmation. If the delay threatens a committed customer milestone, escalate to the supply-chain duty manager and record the reason, owner, and next update time.

For each delayed order, cite the order ID, requested date, estimated date, and the source of the estimate. Reassess the order after a supplier update.

关键词：订单延期、预计交付日期、供应商恢复日期、客户通知、履约风险。
